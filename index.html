Carabao Group wins a major award at Asia CanTech 2025, showing the strength of Thai brands on the world stage

Carabao Group has made Thai industry proud once again by winning a major award at Asia CanTech 2025, the Asia-Pacific conference that brings together can makers and suppliers. The event was held on 6–8 October 2025 in Bangkok.

Special talk on the Carabao brand

One highlight was a special talk by คุณกมลดิษฐ์ สมุทรโคจร, Deputy Managing Director of Carabao Group Public Company Limited. He told the story of the Carabao brand, from its start in Thailand to a global beverage brand, and stressed the group's integrated production and its use of advanced technology to raise product quality to international standards.

The special “Carabao Cup” can

Another highlight this year was the launch of the special “Carabao Cup” can, designed to reflect Carabao Group's role as title sponsor of the Carabao Cup football competition in England. The can carries an eye-catching design with a high-gloss finish that captures the energy, fun and excitement of football. It combines packaging innovation with global marketing strategy.

ACM wins Winner – Beverage Two-Piece Can

For this work, Asia Can Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (ACM), a Carabao Group company, received the Winner – Beverage Two-Piece Can award at the Asia CanTech Awards 2025. The award goes to packaging that stands out for both quality and creativity, and reflects the ability of Thai people to compete on the world stage.

Carabao Group remains committed to taking Thai products to international markets, with confidence in quality, modern design and a firm determination to make Thai brands known and accepted worldwide.

Company overview

Safe beverage packaging, delivered to a customer's hand.

Asia Can Manufacturing Co., Ltd. was established in June 2017 as a joint venture between Carabao Group and Showa Aluminum Can. The company produces aluminum cans as packaging for energy drinks and other beverages.

ACM was born and is driven by the influence of Japanese quality control standards, to deliver safe beverage packaging to customers. Carabao Group has owned 100% of the shares since March 2020.

The factory sits on a 31-rai plot in Carabao Group's state-of-the-art production facilities at Bangpakong, Chachoengsao Province. The site is large enough to install up to two production lines. Today ACM operates a single line capable of approximately 1,000 million cans per year.

Manufacturing

Follow one can through 17 steps, from coil to customer

A two-piece can line designed and installed with advanced equipment from the world's leaders in can line fabrication, and monitored online 24/7.

Process flow

What happens at each step, and what we check

Select a stage or a step. Steps marked CCP are critical control points.

    Printing technology

    Artwork printed, coated, cured and inspected twice.

    Two high-speed decorators print the approved artwork onto dried can bodies. The printed cans receive over varnish on the outside and RIM coating on the bottom, then cure in a pin oven at 190 to 207°C.

    High-resolution 360° cameras inspect the print on every can, and a Mix Label system verifies label and print accuracy again before packing. ACM has produced cans with over a hundred artworks since 2018, in up to eight colours per artwork.

    Cans made to comply with food-contact regulations

    ACM empty aluminum cans are made to comply with the following regulations.

    • Notification of Ministry of Public Health No. 92
    • Japan Food Sanitation Law (JFSL 370)
    • Regulation (EU) 2025/40
    • European Commission Regulation (EU) No 10/2011
    • Department of Medical Sciences announcement on Microbiological Quality Criteria for Food and Food Contact Surfaces (No. 3, B.E. 2560)
    Quality management

    Control at every stage, not only at the end.

    ACM uses first-tier inspection equipment of the kind used across the can manufacturing industry. It is combined with Japanese practice and standards in quality control at every production process, so customers receive cans that are better in quality and safe.

    • Food safety. FSSC 22000, GHPs and HACCP certified.
    • Standards and process. ISO 9001:2015 quality management applied across a 17-step process flow.
    • In-process inspection. A 360° outside camera tester after printing, a light tester for pinholes, an inside camera tester and a Mix Label check, with non-conforming cans rejected automatically.
    • Critical control points. Three CCPs at inside spray, the light tester and the inside camera tester.
    • Online monitoring. 24/7 online monitoring across the line.
